# Westside Community Garden: autumn review notes (meeting, 28 October 2026)

Attendees: Priya (coordinator), Tom (treasurer), Alma (plots), Dev (volunteers), Grace (schools liaison)

Season results
- 42 plots this year, up from 31 in 2025; waitlist of 19 households at season end.
- Harvest logged at the weigh station: 2,860 kg total (2025: 2,140 kg). Tomatoes 610 kg, squash 540 kg, beans 330 kg, greens 410 kg, other 970 kg.
- 1,150 kg went to the Westside food bank, 40% of the harvest, our best share yet.
- Volunteer hours: 1,940 logged, 64 regular volunteers. Saturday work parties averaged 18 people.

Money
- Income 2026: plot fees $8,400, city micro-grant $5,000, plant sale $2,350. Total $15,750.
- Spending: water $3,900 (meter rate went up 18% in May), soil and compost $3,100, tools and repairs $1,600, insurance $1,200, seeds $850. Total $10,650.
- Reserve now $9,800.

Problems
- Water: the north beds dried out twice in the July heatwave; the single tap at the gate cannot serve 42 plots. Drip lines on 12 plots cut their water use by about 35% in a trial.
- Two break-ins at the tool shed (June, September); $700 of tools taken.
- Waitlist frustration: several households have waited two seasons.

Proposals for 2027
- Rainwater: four 1,000 L tanks on the shed and pavilion roofs, about $4,800 installed. Would cover roughly a third of summer watering.
- Extend drip lines to all plots: $2,600 in parts, volunteer-installed over two work parties in April.
- New lockable steel tool cage: $1,900.
- Convert the unused lawn strip on the east side into 10 half-plots for waitlisted households. Needs city sign-off by 15 January.
- Schools: Grace has two classes (grades 3 and 5) interested in a shared teaching bed from May.

Decisions needed at the November general meeting (19 Nov)
- Approve the 2027 budget with rainwater tanks and drip lines ($7,400 from reserve and a new grant application).
- Approve applying to the city for the east strip.
- Raise plot fees from $200 to $220? Tom against; Priya for, to fund water.
